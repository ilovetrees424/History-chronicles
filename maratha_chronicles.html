📜 Maratha History

Origins and Swarajya

The Marathas became a major political and military force in western India during the 17th century. Chhatrapati Shivaji Maharaj established a kingdom based on the idea of Swarajya (self-rule), developed a network of forts, organised administration and used mobile warfare effectively.

Shivaji Maharaj

Shivaji Maharaj was born in 1630 and was crowned at Raigad in 1674. His administration included the Ashtapradhan, a council of eight important offices. He also developed naval capabilities to protect the Konkan coast.

After Shivaji Maharaj

After Shivaji Maharaj, the Maratha state experienced periods of conflict and reorganisation. During the 18th century, the Peshwas became highly influential and Maratha power expanded across large parts of the Indian subcontinent.

Later Period

The Maratha Confederacy consisted of powerful houses including the Scindias, Holkars, Gaekwads and Bhonsles. The Marathas fought three major wars against the British East India Company. The final Anglo-Maratha War ended in 1818, after which British power expanded over former Maratha territories.

👑 Important Leaders

Shivaji Maharaj

Founder of the Maratha Kingdom; crowned in 1674 at Raigad.

Peshwa Balaji Baji Rao

Also known as Nanasaheb; Peshwa during the period leading to Panipat.

Balaji Vishwanath

First Peshwa of the Bhatt family and an important figure in consolidating Maratha politics.

Baji Rao I

Peshwa from 1720–1740, known for rapid cavalry campaigns and expansion.

⚔️ Major Battles & Conflicts

Battle of Pratapgad — 1659

A major confrontation between Shivaji Maharaj's forces and Afzal Khan's army.

Battle of Sinhagad — 1670

Tanaji Malusare led the Maratha assault to recapture Kondhana, later known as Sinhagad.

Third Battle of Panipat — 1761

The Marathas fought Ahmad Shah Durrani's coalition in northern India. The battle had major consequences for 18th-century Indian politics.

Anglo-Maratha Wars

Three major conflicts between the Maratha powers and the British East India Company took place between 1775 and 1818.

🏯 Famous Maratha Forts
